# Provider icons

The Claude WebP and the original Codex WebP were supplied by the project owner
and are used only to identify their respective session providers in LLMPET.
`codex.png` is a cleaned, tightly cropped derivative of that supplied Codex
asset: its baked checkerboard and white tile were removed and replaced with a
real alpha channel. Claude and Codex names and marks remain the property of
their respective owners.

The original download URLs and licenses were not provided with these files. If
the project is redistributed, verify the current trademark and asset-use terms
or replace the files with approved equivalents.

`dsh.svg` is copied from DeepSeek Harness's official
`website/public/favicon.svg` at commit `47f943859bef60e4160492346772ded9b24f765a`.
The upstream repository is Apache-2.0 licensed. The DeepSeek name and mark
remain the property of their owner and are used here only to identify the
corresponding local session provider.
